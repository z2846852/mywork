# Human IPA recordings — source and licensing

Bundled recordings are copies of existing human pronunciation recordings, not synthetic IPA phonemes.
The files are used as examples, not a guarantee of any single accent or error diagnosis.

Source: Nico-Sanchez/schwa, immutable commit 720907443ddd1811756dc4a73b686c774fc277d7
https://github.com/Nico-Sanchez/schwa
37 files (`aa.mp3`, ..., `zh.mp3`) are Schwa's edited (trimmed and loudness-levelled)
Wikimedia Commons samples. The original file, individual creator and licensing
details for EACH sample are recorded at:
https://github.com/Nico-Sanchez/schwa/blob/720907443ddd1811756dc4a73b686c774fc277d7/src/commons.json
The original recordings are licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 (except `dʒ`, public domain).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
For this application the Schwa-prepared audio samples are reused unmodified.
Audio playback volume, speed, and fades are applied at runtime (not re-exported).
This notice covers attribution of the audio, not a change of source-code license.

Additional 22 IPA recordings are retrieved from the pinned GitHub-hosted
Wikimedia Commons transcode mirror eeeeaaii/langkit (commit
b3a08c5e6a4defa272f134503f9937b930c9d2b5):
https://github.com/eeeeaaii/langkit/blob/b3a08c5e6a4defa272f134503f9937b930c9d2b5/audio/ipa/SOURCES.md

The original author, IPA sample description and license for EACH recording
are documented at that URL and its links to Wikimedia Commons. Individual
recordings retain their original Commons licenses (usually CC BY-SA).
The files are transferred as the upstream MP3 transcodes except:
- `retroflex_sh.ogg`: original "Voiceless retroflex sibilant.ogg", by Peter Isotalo,
  and `central_i.ogg`: "Close central unrounded vowel.ogg", by Denelson83.
  These two are re-encoded from the pinned mirror MP3 to Ogg Vorbis for APK
  compatibility. Audio changes: upstream MP3 transcode and build-time conversion.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voiceless_retroflex_sibilant.ogg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_central_unrounded_vowel.ogg

The audio-only derivatives remain subject to the applicable CC BY-SA terms,
including attribution and ShareAlike for distributed derivatives.
No network access is required by the installed App to play these clips.
